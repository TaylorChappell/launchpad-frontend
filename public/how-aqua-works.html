AQUA PROTOCOL GUIDE

How AQUA works

AQUA is a non-custodial Solana launchpad for creating a direct Orca market with a fixed Token-2022 supply, permanently locked opening liquidity and time-weighted holder rewards.

Direct Orca launch, no bonding curve

AQUA does not sell tokens from a bonding curve and does not keep an escrow wallet containing unsold supply. The launch flow creates the mint, metadata, Whirlpool and active concentrated-liquidity position directly. The full configured launch-token allocation is committed to that opening liquidity plan.

What 100% liquidity means

It means the fixed launch-token allocation goes into the opening Orca liquidity position rather than a separate AQUA reserve. It does not mean the pool forever owns every circulating token. As users trade, token and pair-asset reserves move between the Whirlpool and trader wallets. Market price follows the pool state and available liquidity range.

Permanent liquidity lock

AQUA verifies that the opening position is active before permanently locking its position authority. This is intended to prevent the launcher from withdrawing that position. A lock does not guarantee price, volume, depth, uninterrupted trading or that concentrated liquidity remains in range.

Pairs and rewards

The trading pair can be SOL or a supported tokenized-stock asset. Each market records one immutable reward asset. AQUA market cards display the actual paired-asset logo and identify the separate holder reward.

Fees and keeper settlement

The current default Token-2022 transfer fee is 2%: 1% feeds platform allocation and 1% feeds holder rewards. Withheld tokens are not ordinary wallet transfers. The AQUA keeper discovers fee-bearing accounts, harvests withheld tokens and routes or converts the proceeds. Settlement can be delayed or fail and therefore is not promised at an exact frequency.

Time-weighted holder rewards

An eligible balance earns weight for the time it remains held during each 20-minute target epoch. AQUA attempts to allocate every collected amount without an application-level funding minimum; unusable dust carries forward. The interface groups unclaimed epochs by market and displays values in US dollars. A claim unlocks only when the combined reward is worth more than $5 after estimated Solana costs, then wallet-signed proofs deliver the selected reward asset. Opening liquidity and designated protocol accounts are excluded from ordinary holder weighting.

Capped creator alignment

A creator can lock tokens acquired after launch. The quote combines amount and duration. Amount scoring stops at the configured supply target and time scoring stops at the configured maximum duration. The live defaults are a 5% supply target, 365-day maximum scoring duration and 25% maximum share of the 1% platform stream. A tiny lock for 1,000 years therefore stays small: the time input is capped and cannot compensate for a tiny amount.

Charts and data

Market pages display OHLC candlesticks built from indexed market-cap snapshots, not a synthetic price series. Indexing can lag Solana and Orca, so explorers and onchain state remain authoritative.

Cryptoasset risk warning
Cryptoassets are high risk. You could lose all the money you invest, and protections may be limited or unavailable.

Verify before signing

Review the mint, AQUA program, token program, Orca accounts, amounts, fees and destinations in every wallet approval. Solana transactions are generally irreversible.
