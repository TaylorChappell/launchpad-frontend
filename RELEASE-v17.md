# AQUA frontend v17 — visible costs and creator controls

- Shows the fixed 0.02 SOL AQUA fee and a clear estimated total launch-cost range before wallet approval.
- States that Solana/Orca rent and network charges are estimates and that the wallet confirmation is authoritative.
- Adds creator-only token controls for fee-share quotes, creator locks, matured lock release, and outstanding fee claims.
- Makes clear that only tokens bought after launch can be creator-locked; the original supply stays in permanent Orca liquidity.
- Shows whether automatic holder-reward epochs are enabled and keeps existing on-chain reward claims.
- Updates the fee flywheel to the capped creator/treasury/buyback allocation.
